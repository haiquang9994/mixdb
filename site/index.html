MixDB

A desktop client for the things you keep open beside your editor.

  • Database — MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis, over SSH tunnels when you need them.
  • REST — compose requests, read responses, keep history and per-environment variables.
  • Terminal — a shell on this machine, or on a server over SSH.
  • Tools — the small conversions and generators you reach for mid-task.
